PRODUCTS
Are your Pokémon and anime products authentic?

That is the standard of the store. We intend to list genuine products sourced through legitimate Japanese retail or resale channels and will not knowingly sell counterfeit or resealed items.

Are you affiliated with Pokémon, Nintendo, or anime studios?

No. Bringing Japan 2 U is an independent retailer. Trademarks, characters and brand names belong to their respective owners.

What does “Japan sourced” mean?

It means the item was obtained from Japanese retail, distribution or resale channels. Individual listings may provide more detail where it matters, especially for collector products.

SHIPPING
Where do you ship?

Final destination availability and shipping rates will be confirmed before checkout goes live. Some products may have destination restrictions due to size, fragility or carrier rules.

Will my order have tracking?

Tracked service will be used when available for the selected shipping method. The order confirmation will show the service used and any tracking number provided by the carrier.

What about customs or import fees?

International orders can be subject to destination-country taxes, duties or handling fees. Those charges, when applicable, are generally determined by local authorities or carriers rather than the store.

ORDERS
Can I buy from the site today?

Not yet. The current site is a pre-launch preview. The availability inquiry list works for testing interest, but no payment is collected until pricing, inventory and secure checkout are finalized.

What if an item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact us promptly with your order information and clear photos of the item and packaging. Final return/refund timelines will be stated in the launch policy before payment is enabled.

Are collectible returns different?

They can be. Sealed collectibles, trading cards and opened products may require special return rules because condition can change after delivery. Those rules will be clearly disclosed before checkout launches.

SOURCING REQUESTS
Can I ask you to find something specific in Japan?

Yes. Send the item name, brand/series, your approximate budget and any reference link or image description. A request is not a purchase commitment; it helps us evaluate future sourcing.

Can you guarantee you'll find my requested item?

No. Availability, condition and pricing in Japan can change quickly, especially for limited collectibles. We will only quote or list an item once we can reasonably confirm the product and terms.
