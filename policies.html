01

Authenticity

Bringing Japan 2 U intends to sell genuine goods sourced through legitimate Japanese retail, distribution or resale channels. We do not knowingly sell counterfeit products. Where collector condition, edition or source materially affects a purchase, the listing should disclose it.

We are an independent retailer and are not sponsored by or affiliated with Pokémon, Nintendo, anime studios, publishers, character licensors or other brands unless a future listing explicitly states otherwise.

02

Shipping

Available destinations, carrier options, shipping cost and estimated delivery windows will be shown or confirmed before payment. Delivery estimates are not guarantees and may be affected by customs, weather, carrier delays or destination-country processing.

International customers may be responsible for local duties, taxes or carrier handling charges where applicable.

03

Returns & order issues

Final return windows and procedures will be published before checkout launches. If an order arrives damaged, materially different from the listing or incorrect, customers should contact us promptly and retain the shipping packaging and product for review.

04

Collectible condition

Trading cards, sealed products and collectible packaging can have minor manufacturing or distribution imperfections. Listings will distinguish sealed products from singles and will provide condition information when it is relevant. Opening a sealed product can affect return eligibility under the final launch policy.

Randomized products such as booster packs do not guarantee specific cards, rarities or pull rates unless the manufacturer explicitly guarantees them.

05

Sourcing requests

A sourcing request is an expression of interest, not an order or guarantee. We may be unable to locate an item, or the available price/condition may differ from the request. Any sourced item will require clear product details and customer approval before payment.

06

Privacy & payments

Do not send card numbers or other sensitive payment details through email, Instagram or TikTok. When checkout launches, payment will be handled through a secure payment provider. A final privacy notice describing information collected by the live store will be published before checkout is enabled.
