WHY WE EXIST

We miss the feeling of finding it in Japan.

Bringing Japan 2 U is being built for the moment when you see something in a shop, station, Pokémon Center, character store, hobby aisle, ceramic shelf or tiny corner of Japan and think: I need that.

THE IDEA

Not “Japanese-themed.”
Actually Japan-first.

The goal is not to build a generic ecommerce catalog and decorate it with red circles and kanji. The product should have a reason to be here: Japanese edition, Japanese retail context, design, licensing, availability, culture, or simply a discovery you’re unlikely to make at home.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

The rules before the inventory.

We’re setting the standard while the store is still small, because that’s easier than trying to repair trust later.

01

Say exactly what it is.

Pack bundle, sealed box, single card, concept product—those are different things. The listing should make the distinction obvious.

02

Don’t fake scale.

A focused shelf of real inventory is better than a giant storefront full of placeholder products pretending to be available.

03

Make Japan matter.

If the product could come from anywhere with no meaningful difference, it should not become the identity of the store.

04

Let customers shape the hunt.

Requests are product research. The things people actually ask for should influence what gets sourced next.
